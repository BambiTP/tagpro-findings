# Players: rating leaderboard

The top 150 players by our own rating among players with 300+ rated games since January 2025
(public and ranked, from tagpro.eu). How the rating works and how it was tested is in
[FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md) section 1.

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| Rating | Our team-Elo rating after every finished public and ranked game on tagpro.eu up to 16 September 2026 (start 1500). Only registered names are rated |
| Career rated games | Games counted in the rating since 2015 |
| Games / win rate since 2025 | 4v4 public and ranked games since January 2025 |
| Beyond rating | How many percentage points per game the player's teams won above what the rating gap predicted. Only partly reliable: a player's value in one half of their games predicts the other half with correlation 0.24 |
| Defense style | From ranked replays: how far the player sits from their flag when defending under attack, compared with the map average, as a percentile (higher = more active). "–" means fewer than 11 ranked games defending |

Caveats: unregistered players in a game are treated as an average 1500 player; casual public games
and ranked games count the same; players who use several names are split across them; and players who
stopped playing keep their last rating (only players active since 2025 are listed).

Note on maps: we also checked whether players do better on particular maps. They don't, measurably:
how a player does on a map in half their games does not predict the other half (findings section 7).
