# Players: best by scoreboard score

Who scores highest on the in-game scoreboard over many ranked games, split by role. Built from
11,237 clean ranked capture-the-flag games (March 2025 to October 2026). Why the
boards are split, and what the score does and does not measure, is in [SCOREBOARD.md](SCOREBOARD.md).

**Read this first.**
- **This ranks the scoreboard score, not skill or contribution.** The score only sees caps, hold,
  captures per grab, returns, prevent and tags. Blocking, regrabbing, anti-regrab and powerups count
  for nothing. For a skill rating, see [PLAYERS.md](PLAYERS.md).
- **Offence and defence are never compared with each other.** A player's game counts as an offence
  game when their offence number beat their defence number that game, otherwise as a defence game.
  Offence players score lower overall (see SCOREBOARD.md), so mixing them would bury them.
- **Scores are relative to each lobby.** 50 is that game's average. Someone who mostly plays in
  strong lobbies is compared against strong players every game, so their average is held down.
- **Being on good teams helps.** Captures, hold and prevent all come more easily on a winning team,
  and the score does not correct for that.
- **Likely range** is where the player's true average probably sits (a 95% range). Neighbouring
  players whose ranges overlap are not clearly different.
- **Ranked only, one name per account.** Names are each account's most recent ranked name.

How repeatable each board is (each player's average in half their games against the other half; 1
would mean perfectly repeatable): offence 0.60, defence 0.87, overall 0.89.

**Our rating** is the skill rating from [PLAYERS.md](PLAYERS.md): a team Elo built from every
finished public and ranked game on tagpro.eu since 2015 (start 1500), as of 16 September 2026. It is
matched to the board by name, so players whose ranked name differs from their registered tagpro.eu
name show as "not rated" (10 players: CRYCHIC, Void., `+.*-', cracktheroni, may_hem, oldAlt, opaque, traddles, tremor, y).
